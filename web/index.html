Summary

The same evidence, moved

Language models are known to answer better when the evidence they need sits at the start or the end of a long context. That result was established on transformers. It is usually read as a property of long context itself, and increasingly quoted about models that are not transformers at all.

We test that reading directly. For each of 800 multi-document questions we move a single answer-bearing passage through ten positions while holding the question, the nine distractors, the prompt template, and the decoding configuration fixed. Everything that changes is where the evidence sits. We run that intervention across 17 pinned checkpoints spanning attention, state-space, and hybrid mixers.

The U-shape is not one phenomenon. At matched 2.8B scale on the same corpus, Pythia carries a clear primacy arm; Mamba and Mamba-2 carry none. All three carry a recency arm of similar size. The middle-of-context penalty that gets quoted as universal is, in this comparison, two separable effects with different scopes: recency travels across mixers, primacy does not.

Primacy effect at matched 2.8B scale, in percentage points, over 800 questions with 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples. Paired Pythia-minus-Mamba differences are +5.31 and +7.00 points, both Holm p < 0.0001.

Intervention

One passage, ten positions, nothing else moved

The unit of analysis is the question, not the generation. Every bootstrap resample draws complete bundles of all ten positions together, so the interval covers variation across questions rather than across independently sampled prompts.

Primacy is mean accuracy at positions 1 and 2 minus mean accuracy at positions 5 and 6. Recency is mean accuracy at positions 9 and 10 minus mean accuracy at positions 5 and 6. Inference uses 10,000 bootstrap resamples of complete question bundles with Holm correction across the two edge tests. The 2,655-question release was split once with seed 240521 into 800 exploratory and 1,855 confirmatory questions; every number on this page comes from the exploratory allocation.

Main result

Position curves by sequence mixer

Switch between the matched architecture comparison, the matched 8B attention control, the corpus control, and the production systems. Colour encodes the mixer class: attention, state-space, hybrid.

The matched 8B panel is the closest thing here to a causal test, and it does not settle the question. Both checkpoints share training data, tokenizer, scale, depth, and positional-encoding setup, and the hybrid replaces about 7% of Mamba-2 blocks with attention. The hybrid has a significant within-model primacy edge and the pure model does not, but the paired hybrid-minus-pure difference is +1.88 points with a 95% interval of [−0.56, +4.44] and Holm p = 0.1442. The direction agrees with the primary comparison. The evidence does not establish that attention caused it.

Task transfer

Does it survive off multi-document QA?

Half of it does. The transformer primacy arm reappears on synthetic needle retrieval; the state-space models saturate and cannot be compared there.

Calibration

Proving the instrument can see the effect

A null result is only informative if the measurement could have detected a real effect. Before any model comparison, the harness was shown to recover a known position effect end to end.

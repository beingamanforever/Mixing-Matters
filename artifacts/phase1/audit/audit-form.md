# Audit form

Record at most one failure category per item, or none if the response is correct.

- formatting: the answer is correct but wrapped in extra text or punctuation the grader could not parse.
- extraction: the model copied the wrong span or fact from the supplied context.
- hallucination: the model invented an answer unsupported by the context or closed-book knowledge.
- truncation: the response was cut off before a complete answer was produced.
